For your Plex library

Find themes for movies, TV shows, and collections. Themerr supports Plex Movie and Plex Series agents and can respond to library updates as new titles arrive.

For your Jellyfin library

Connect your server and choose your libraries. Themerr installs its matching Jellyfin connector and can scan for theme music on a schedule.

From setup to soundtrack

How Themerr works

  1. 01

    Connect your server

    Run Themerr alongside Plex or Jellyfin and choose the libraries you want to give theme music.

  2. 02

    Find a theme

    Themerr matches titles against the community-maintained ThemerrDB. Explore matches and preview themes in the app.

  3. 03

    Keep it in sync

    Respond to Plex library updates or schedule scans to add theme music to your growing collection.
